Working with me

I am always glad to hear from students interested in black hole accretion, GRMHD simulation, ray-tracing or gravitational-wave phenomenology — whether for a summer project, a master's thesis, or simply to talk through an idea. Projects can be weighted toward analytic modelling, large-scale simulation, or the interpretation of observational data, depending on what you want to learn.
