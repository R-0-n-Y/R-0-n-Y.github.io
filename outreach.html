Outreach & science communication

Roughly a hundred papers appear on astro-ph every day. Most of them never reach anyone outside a narrow specialism. A fair amount of my spare energy goes into fixing that.

Today's Research Minutes

I founded and write a newsletter that curates and summarises papers from astro-ph for students and the wider public — short, readable digests of what came out and why it matters, aimed at people who want to follow the field without reading forty abstracts a morning.
